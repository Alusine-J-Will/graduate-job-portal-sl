## 1. Introduction

Graduate Job Portal SL (GradConnect SL) is a web-based platform designed to connect graduates, employers, and administrators in a simple and efficient online environment. The system enables graduates to create professional profiles, search for suitable job opportunities, and apply for jobs. Employers can register their companies, post vacancies, review applications, and manage applicants. Administrators are responsible for overseeing system operations, managing users, monitoring jobs, reviewing applications, and generating reports.

The main purpose of this system is to simplify recruitment and employment processes for graduates and employers in Sierra Leone. It provides a centralized digital platform where job seekers can access career opportunities and employers can find suitable candidates.

The target users of the system include:

- Graduates seeking employment opportunities
- Employers looking for qualified candidates
- Administrators managing the platform

The major features of the system include:

- User registration and login
- Graduate profile management
- Employer company profile management
- Job posting and management
- Job search and filtering
- Job applications
- Notifications
- Admin dashboard and reporting tools

## 3. Getting Started

### 3.1 Accessing the System

To access the system, open a web browser and enter the application URL. Once the homepage opens, users can choose to register or log in depending on their role.

### 3.2 Registration

New users must create an account before using the system.

- Click the Register button on the homepage
- Select the appropriate account type:
  - Graduate
  - Employer
- Complete the registration form
- Submit the form

### 3.3 Login

After registration, users can log in by:

- Entering their email address
- Entering their password
- Clicking the Login button

### 3.4 Logout

To log out safely:

- Click the Logout option from the navigation menu
- The system will end the current session

## 4. Graduate User Guide

### 4.1 Register an Account

To register as a graduate:

1. Open the system homepage.
2. Click Register.
3. Select Graduate as the account type.
4. Fill in personal details such as first name, last name, email, phone number, and password.
5. Confirm the password.
6. Accept the terms and conditions.
7. Click Create Account.

### 4.2 Login

To log in:

1. Open the login page.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Enter your password.
4. Click Login.

### 4.3 Complete Profile

A complete profile improves the chances of being noticed by employers.
To complete your profile:

1. Go to the Graduate Dashboard.
2. Open the Profile section.
3. Enter your personal information.
4. Add your location and short biography.
5. Save the changes.

### 4.4 Add Education

To add education details:

1. Open the Education section.
2. Click Add Education.
3. Enter the institution name.
4. Select or enter the degree.
5. Enter the field of study.
6. Enter graduation year.
7. Save the record.

### 4.5 Add Experience

To add work experience:

1. Open the Experience section.
2. Click Add Experience.
3. Enter the organization name.
4. Enter your position.
5. Add start and end dates.
6. Add a short description.
7. Save the record.

### 4.6 Add Skills

To add skills:

1. Open the Skills section.
2. Enter one or more relevant skills.
3. Save the changes.

### 4.7 Upload Photo

To upload a profile photo:

1. Open the Profile section.
2. Select Choose File.
3. Upload a clear profile image.
4. Save the changes.

### 4.8 Upload CV

To upload a CV:

1. Go to the Upload CV section.
2. Choose your CV file.
3. Upload the file.
4. Confirm that the file has been added successfully.

### 4.9 Search Jobs

To search for jobs:

1. Open the Jobs page.
2. Use the search bar to enter keywords.
3. Apply filters such as category, location, or job type.
4. Click Search.

### 4.10 View Job Details

To view job details:

1. Click on a selected job listing.
2. Review the full job description, requirements, and deadline.
3. Decide whether the position matches your profile.

### 4.11 Apply for Jobs

To apply for a job:

1. Open the job details page.
2. Click Apply.
3. Confirm that your profile and CV are available.
4. Submit the application.

### 4.12 Track Applications

To track applications:

1. Open the Applications page.
2. View the status of each submitted application.
3. Review whether the application is pending, shortlisted, reviewed, or accepted.

### 4.13 View Notifications

To view notifications:

1. Click the Notification Bell.
2. Open the Notification Center.
3. Review recent alerts and updates.
4. Click View to access the relevant page.

### 4.14 Update Profile

To update profile information:

1. Open the Profile page.
2. Edit the required fields.
3. Save the changes.

## 5. Employer User Guide

### 5.1 Register a Company Account

To register as an employer:

1. Open the homepage.
2. Click Register.
3. Select Employer as the account type.
4. Fill in company details such as company name, contact person, industry, email, phone number, and password.
5. Submit the form.

### 5.2 Login

To log in as an employer:

1. Open the login page.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your password.
4. Click Login.

### 5.3 Complete Company Profile

To complete the company profile:

1. Open the Employer Dashboard.
2. Go to the Company Profile section.
3. Enter company information.
4. Save the profile.

### 5.4 Post Jobs

To post a new job:

1. Open the Post a Job page.
2. Enter the job title.
3. Add the job description.
4. Enter job requirements.
5. Select the appropriate category.
6. Set the deadline.
7. Click Submit.

### 5.5 Edit Jobs

To edit an existing job:

1. Open Manage Jobs.
2. Select the relevant job.
3. Click Edit.
4. Update the required information.
5. Save the changes.

### 5.6 Delete Jobs

To delete a job:

1. Open Manage Jobs.
2. Select the job to remove.
3. Click Delete.
4. Confirm the action.

### 5.7 Manage Jobs

Employers can manage all posted jobs from the Manage Jobs page. This includes:

- Viewing active jobs
- Editing job listings
- Deleting outdated jobs
- Checking job status

### 5.8 View Applicants

To view applicants:

1. Open the Applicants page.
2. Review the list of applicants.
3. Click on an applicant to see more details.

### 5.9 Review Applications

To review applications:

1. Open the applicant details page.
2. Review the graduate’s profile, CV, and application details.
3. Decide the next action.

### 5.10 Update Application Status

To update an application status:

1. Open the application review page.
2. Select a status such as Submitted, Under Review, Shortlisted, Accepted, or Rejected.
3. Save the changes.

### 5.11 View Notifications

Employers can view application updates and system alerts through the Notification Center. Notifications may include:

- New applications
- Application status updates
- Job alerts
- Admin messages

## 8. Troubleshooting Guide

### 8.1 Cannot Login

Possible causes:

- Incorrect email address
- Incorrect password
- Account not activated
- Account disabled

Suggested solution:

- Check the email and password
- Reset the password if necessary
- Contact the administrator if the account status is pending or inactive

### 8.2 Invalid Password

Suggested solution:

- Re-enter the password carefully
- Use the password reset feature if available
- Ensure the Caps Lock key is off

### 8.3 Job Not Visible

Possible causes:

- Job has expired
- Search filters are too strict
- The job was not published successfully

Suggested solution:

- Check the job status
- Remove search filters
- Refresh the page

### 8.4 Application Not Submitted

Possible causes:

- Required profile information is missing
- CV is not uploaded
- The application form was incomplete

Suggested solution:

- Complete the profile before applying
- Upload the CV
- Re-submit the application

### 8.5 Notification Issues

Possible causes:

- Notification list is empty
- The notification was already marked as read
- The browser has not refreshed

Suggested solution:

- Refresh the page
- Check the notification center
- Re-open the dashboard

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I register an account?
   You can register by clicking Register on the homepage and selecting your user type.

2. Can I register as both a graduate and an employer?
   No. Each user should register with the role that matches their purpose.

3. What should I do if I forget my password?
   Use the password recovery option if available, or contact the system administrator.

4. How do I update my profile?
   Go to your profile page and edit the required information, then save the changes.

5. Can I apply for more than one job?
   Yes, graduates can apply for multiple jobs as long as they meet the requirements.

6. Can employers edit a job after posting it?
   Yes, employers can edit existing job listings from the manage jobs section.

7. What if my application has not been reviewed?
   Please wait for the employer or administrator to update the application status.

8. How do I view notifications?
   Click the notification bell or open the notification center from the dashboard.

9. Can administrators remove inappropriate jobs?
   Yes, administrators can manage and review job postings.

10. Is the system available on mobile devices?
    Yes, the system can be accessed on mobile phones, tablets, and desktops.

11. What if I cannot upload my CV?
    Ensure the file format is supported and the file size is not too large.

12. How can I know whether my application was successful?
    Check the application status in the Applications section or notification center.
